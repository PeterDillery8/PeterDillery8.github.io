About

I am a research assistant at Bonn, working with Jessica Fintzen. I study the local and global Langlands correspondence, with recent projects touching related aspects of algebraic geometry. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Michigan in 2022 (advisor: Tasho Kaletha). From Fall 2022 to Fall 2025 I was a Brin Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Maryland, where I co-organized the UMD Number Theory and Representation Theory Seminar and taught for the DC Math Circle.
